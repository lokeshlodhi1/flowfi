Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FlowFi. Your privacy is important to us. FlowFi is designed as an offline-first personal finance application that helps you manage expenses, income, budgets and financial records while giving you full control over your data.

2. Information You Store

FlowFi allows you to store information such as transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, notes, subscriptions, reminders and password manager entries. This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to create a backup.

3. Local Data Storage

FlowFi is an offline-first application. Your financial records are stored in the application's local database on your device. We do not upload your financial information to our own servers.

4. Google Drive Backup

FlowFi provides an optional Google Drive Backup feature. When enabled by you, an encrypted backup file is uploaded to your own Google Drive account. FlowFi does not keep copies of these backups on its own servers. You may delete your backups at any time from your Google Drive.

5. Manual Device Backup

You can also export a manual backup file and save it anywhere on your device or external storage. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files.

6. Password Manager

Password Manager data is stored locally on your device. If you choose to include Password Manager data during backup, it becomes part of your encrypted backup file stored in your Google Drive account.

7. Premium Purchases

Premium plans are prepaid and processed securely through Razorpay. FlowFi does not store your debit card number, credit card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net banking password or any other payment credentials.

Premium plans do not renew automatically. There are no recurring charges or automatic deductions. Users must manually purchase or renew a plan after it expires.

8. Permissions

  • Storage: Import and export backup files.
  • Internet: Google Drive backup, payment verification and app services.
  • Notifications: Bill reminders and scheduled alerts.
  • Biometric Authentication (optional): Protect Password Manager.

9. Third-Party Services

FlowFi may use Google Drive APIs for backup, Razorpay for payment processing and Google Play Services where required. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Rights

You may delete your local data, remove Google Drive backups, uninstall the application or stop using backup features at any time.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our website.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us.
