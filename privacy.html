Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Your localized data privacy must remain strictly confidential. This transparency document discloses exactly how ExpenseMaster Pro isolates individual data structures.

1. Local Storage Enclosure

ExpenseMaster Pro operates as a strict offline application. We never monitor, copy, serialize, aggregate, or broadcast private bookkeeping inputs, revenue parameters, tag identifiers, or currency quantities to cloud databases. Your database lives purely on your physical smartphone storage.

2. File Management Clearances

The system utilizes file directory writing authorizations solely to store encrypted data outputs or structured CSV sheets to storage blocks. These functions run directly under explicit manual control by the device owner.

3. External Modules and Security

No telemetry tracking modules, commercial metric software, or marketing tracking pixels run within this application runtime environment. Your financial logs are entirely sandboxed, avoiding cross-profile monetization or metadata extraction risks.

4. Verification Channel

If you have any specific regulatory questions or require verification of our offline file-handling configurations, feel free to reach out to our engineering inbox directly at:
